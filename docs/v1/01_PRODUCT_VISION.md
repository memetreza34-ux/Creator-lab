# Product Vision

## Who V1 is for

Primary: serious solo creators and small creator brands that publish repeatedly and want a system for improving output, not only a scheduling utility.

Secondary: very small teams that can operate without complex approval, permission, or agency workflows.

Not V1: large agencies, enterprise social teams, social listening teams, customer-support inbox teams.

## Positioning

Creator Lab is a content operating system for creators.

It should combine:
- content planning and pipeline
- strong multi-destination composer
- scheduling and publishing
- creator analytics
- evidence-based AI interpretation
- structured experiments
- a learning loop that improves future decisions

## Product principles

1. One connected workflow beats many isolated tools.
2. Real data must be visually and semantically distinct from mock/demo data.
3. AI gives diagnostic assistance and hypotheses, not guarantees of virality.
4. Insights must show evidence, sample size, and confidence where applicable.
5. The creator stays in control; AI suggestions are reviewable before application.
6. Platform-specific constraints are first-class domain rules.
7. Scheduling is infrastructure, not the long-term moat.
8. Creator-specific learning is the long-term moat.

## V1 promise

"Plan, create, publish, measure, and learn what works for your content from one workspace."

## Long-term direction

Creator Lab should learn patterns such as effective hooks, formats, lengths, topics, posting windows, and CTAs for a specific creator. Full Content DNA is deliberately beyond V1 until enough real historical data exists.
